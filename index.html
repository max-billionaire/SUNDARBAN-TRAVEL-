Boat Trips

Choose a short river experience or ask us about a longer journey. Minimum boat ride duration is 2 hours.

Boat ride options

  • Hourly boat ride — minimum 2 hours
  • Full-day boat ride
  • Boat booking for a tour package
  • Duration choices: 2, 3, 4 hours or custom hours

Pickup points: Jharkhali Tiger Rescue Center or Jharkhali Bazar.

Boat hire rates

6C boat₹1,300 / hour
4C boat₹1,100 / hour
2C boat₹900 / hour

6C / 4C / 2C are boat types, not passenger-capacity labels. Rates shown are boat hire rates per hour.

Enquire about a boat trip →

Permissions & guide

  • Forest visitor permission: ₹200 per person
  • Boat permission: ₹1,000 fixed per boat
  • One government-required guide is mandatory for every boat

Guide cost is separate and will be confirmed by the team. Permission charges are separate from boat hire.
